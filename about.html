I am a postdoctoral fellow associated with the Department of Physics and the Quantum Science and Engineering Center (QSEC) at Indiana University, Bloomington. I am jointly hosted by Profs. Babak Seradjeh, Ceren B. Dag and Debayan Mitra. I earned my Ph.D. from Indiana University in 2026, after completing an integrated Bachelors and Masters degree in Engineering Physics from the Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi, India in 2020.

My work lies at the interface of theoretical condensed matter and atomic, molecular and optical (AMO) physics. Broadly, my work spans topological phases, periodically-driven (Floquet) systems and quantum simulation of many-body physics on AMO platforms. I am also interested in developing tensor network based methods to study strongly correlated systems, topologically-ordered phases and quantum error correction.
